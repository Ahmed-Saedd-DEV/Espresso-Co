The Terroir of Gedeo Zone

Cultivated on smallholder plots nestled along the mist-clad highlands of Yirgacheffe, this lot originates from wild Indigenous Heirloom varietals growing under native shade canopies. Ripe cherries are selectively hand-picked at dawn, depulped on the same day, and cold-fermented in pristine natural mountain spring water for 36 hours before sun-drying on raised African beds.

In the cup, it exhibits extraordinary floral aromatic lift—reminiscent of white jasmine gardenia—balanced against bright candied white peach and sparkling bergamot tea notes.
